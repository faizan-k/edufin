Planning Methodology & Mathematical Principles

1. Staggered Drawdown Math

Traditional planners erroneously sum all 4 years of tuition as if needed upfront. In reality, tuition for year 4 is only withdrawn 3 years after college begins. By keeping later funds invested in high-grade liquid bonds at 5%, the required matriculation corpus is discounted by:

PV = NetCost / (1 + 0.05)^k

2. 3-Year Glide Path

Equities offer 12% long-term historical returns but carry severe sequence-of-returns risk if a bear market strikes right when your child turns 17. The 3-Year Glide Path automatically shifts assets into capital preservation (7% return) at age 15 to lock in gains.

3. Destination Inflation & Living

Education inflation outpaces CPI inflation (historically 6-9%). When sending children to the US, UK, or Canada, foreign exchange and overseas living costs add a 30% premium. We model both factor combinations dynamically.
